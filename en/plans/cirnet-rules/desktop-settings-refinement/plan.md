# Desktop Settings refinement plan

[Acceptance and verification](../../../specs/cirnet-rules/desktop-settings-refinement/spec.md)

1. Verify the existing scrollbar and wheel behavior. Centralize hover visibility, wheel routing, search field decoration, and palette styling in the Qt theme.
2. Refine Appearance, Archived chats, and Storage. Load a chat-only storage summary through the runtime, independently of Settings navigation.
3. Verify editor input, navigation, storage scope, archive mutations, persistence, and the light and dark layouts with disposable data.
