# English authority and translations

Maintain matching English and Vietnamese paths, document IDs, revisions, critical anchors and machine identifiers. Record the normalized English SHA-256 in Vietnamese metadata. Update both in one change. Hash checks do not perform semantic review. Review must compare modality, versions, safety and release conditions using the [glossary](../references/glossary.md).
